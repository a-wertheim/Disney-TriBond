The Game Shelf

Party games that run in a browser tab. Open one on a laptop or cast it to the television, hand the room a single screen, and play. Nothing to download, no accounts, no scores leaving your device.

How these work

Everything runs in the page itself. There is no server, no database and no analytics — the only thing that gets saved is your solo best score, kept in your own browser and never sent anywhere. Close the tab and the game is over; open it again and your best score is still there.

Each game follows your system light or dark setting and has its own toggle. They work on a phone, but a laptop or a television is a better time when more than one person is playing.
